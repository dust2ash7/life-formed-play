    Dark rock in the water. Moss shows up in the damp crack. Do not stack these into a wall.

    Fist-size · Always wet · Leave it

In the channel

  • California bulrush

    Schoenoplectus californicus

    The tall green tube that stands in the channel and slows the flow.

    6–10 ft · Standing water · Plug

  • Southern cattail

    Typha domingensis

    Blades in the slow edge of the bed. Same plant as the bank list. Not up on the dry shelf.

    6–9 ft · Slow edge · Plug

  • Pale spikerush

    Eleocharis macrostachya

    A fine rush in the shallows. This is the reed on the shelf.

    1–3 ft · Shallows · Plug

  • Mosquito fern

    Azolla filiculoides

    A floating skin on still pools. It turns red in full sun. It does not belong in a riffle.

    A skin · Still pools · Let it come

Do not pave the bed or dump fill in it. Bulrush, cattail, and spikerush go in as plugs where water already stands, November through February. Leave mosquito fern to still pools. Do not plant giant reed or watercress. Watercress is the white-flowered mat in a lot of these creeks. It is not native. Pull it. Do not spray the water.
